## Newly source-proven causal lifecycle mismatch
`GameActivity.onDraw` previously captured `int lifeStamp=episode` **before** `updateHabitat(slice)`. The latter may call `extendHabitat()` when the wild world has fewer than three objects and phase >=4. That routine executes `episode++` **without a new physical life**, without `brain3.newLife()` and without aborting the current V319 action. The same frame then calls genuine `brain3.actuate(...)` / V319 `originalTimestepV356` + `actuate`, setting `hasStep=true`. After real solver and object contacts, the normal `brain3.observed(...)` / V319 `observe` is guarded by `lifeStamp==episode`. The streaming increment makes that false, silently skipping real POST. On the next frame `originalTimestepV356` rejects `hasStep=true`. This is a source-proven PRE/POST pairing defect, independent of speculative motor policy or rewards. Whether it explains **every** historical crash must still be established by real Android 10k tests.
