4. **Original 2k QA used `LifeStatistics.encode()` last-50 completed lives** as a supposed full-lifetime red tally. At >50 deaths the early harmful contacts permanently disappear; this gives a demonstrably false decreasing red total in 10k long-run. Fixed in V406 10k test: read genuine `EnergyLifetimeCounters.encode()` (original `elt1|blue|red|violet|legacy|complete` full-lifetime monotonic contact counters) via a read-only GameActivity QA accessor. The 50-life UI history stays unchanged. Independent Android regression simulates 81 real deaths and proves 31 harmful contacts used to disappear from the old measurement. End-to-end all 10k buckets strictly require red/blue/violet/weight-update monotonicity.
5. **Original V356 saved-brain loader arbitrarily rejects a 100,001-decision trained GRU** because `Zero3OriginalBrainV356.inspectSaved` checked `count>100000` even though serialized `count` is `long` and V319 has an `int` decision journal. Source correction in separate V407: allow all nonnegative original checkpoint counts strictly below `Integer.MAX_VALUE`, with unchanged exact V356 CRC, schema, 194 senses, GRU64, 512 cortex weights, V319, 12 slots.
6. **V319 hand journal credit validator multiplies 2 ints**, `credits>decisions*SECTORS`; after `134217727` decisions multiplication overflows signed 32-bit and can reject a valid grip checkpoint. Separate V407 casts `(long)decisions*SECTORS`. Future-safe long-run, unchanged saved binary layout.
7. **V402 original saved S21 raw COM vs measured PRE inertia**: [Android 4-arm matched 16-situation run 40728656362](https://github.com/nicogtr20-ctrl/idle-runner-releases/actions/runs/40728656362) increased mean gain from +.046417 raw to +.056981 but worsened actual wall+SOUTH by -.070198; EAST still moves WEST -2.477. 8. **V405 genuine 2k red forensic encountered zero red** [run 40730624733](https://github.com/nicogtr20-ctrl/idle-runner-releases/actions/runs/40730624733). 2k cannot attribute hazard avoidance or establish chooser vs physical drift.
