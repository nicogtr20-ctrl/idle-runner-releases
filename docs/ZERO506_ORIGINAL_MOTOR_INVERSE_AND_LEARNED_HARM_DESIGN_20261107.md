gravity hack, persistent hidden controller or user data reset.

surface geometry, COM position and COM velocity. For each of the original

## Real-contact learned harm confidence

`Zero3InstrumentalCreditV371` under opt-in V505 uses only wavelengths whose
physical membrane contact produced actual energy damage on at least two
distinct accepted experiences, or a weaker authentic legacy prior.
It measures current observed spectrum and the alignment of BOTH:
(a) the issued genuine GRU heading, and (b) an independently verified
motor-forecast physical bearing when available. The larger exposure adds
a conservative *risk cost* to the same V353 score. It never labels
a wavelength as inherently harmful or rewarding, prescribes a safe
direction, injects a future collision reward, or rewrites a sensor.

Crucial: learned harm also applies when motor model is not
heldout-reliable and V371 would otherwise return early during drift.
No risk score exists before experienced harm. It is an opt-in sensory
loss aversion, not a claimed independent second energy observation.
