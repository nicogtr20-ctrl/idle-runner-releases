## Intent and boundaries
The request was to let ZERO distinguish its own motion from environmental change, notice that previously obstructed space may become passable, and create/verify predictions through genuinely different actions, without supplying any "open gate" script. We added a **small empirically learned causal-experiment approximation**, NOT semantic understanding, a hidden world oracle or consciousness.

## Implementation
New independent `CausalExperiments.java`, pref key `causal_experiments_v111`, versioned `ce1` validated encoding:
- During a real `SensorimotorMind.choose()`, save a **13×13 odometry-aligned snapshot** of the already observed 36-unit spatial cells and the selected action. Keep the currently observed obstacle-distance context in three generic sensor bands (near/mid/far), not predefined object identities or gate positions. Important: randomized action selection in the pre-existing exploration branch is recorded as a limited intervention.
- At the next real physical decision boundary (~0.65 **simulated** seconds) compare exactly the same grid cells against the new observed `SpatialPerception` map and the nucleus' physically integrated body displacement. **Egomotion alone does not count as a moved obstacle.** An environmental change requires previous occupied evidence >=2 to become observed free, or free <=-2 to become observed blocked. Newly observed free (old unknown) is counted separately; it is not asserted to be a moving object.
- When previously occupied space is seen free, record a **provisional opening** (generic grid key, not a recognized gate). Mark **actual traversability** only when the real body subsequently travels into this cell with sufficient physical movement; the action associated with the candidate gets retrospective learned utility. This is an imperfect proxy based on grid evidence, not a formal causal proof.
- Maintain learned observation-conditioned movement, environmental-change and consequence statistics for 16 motor actions × 3 sensory contexts (**48** different empirical action situations), compared against the average of actions in the same context. Actual energy gained/lost is received only from `SensorimotorMind.contactOutcome`, alongside the existing lived movement feedback.
- **Guarded use of hypotheses:** a new model initially makes no change whatsoever to ZERO's decisions. It must accumulate at least **30 out-of-training tests**, predict subsequent movement/environment effects at least **5% better** than an action-independent same-context reference, and observe **three randomized trials of the candidate action and at least five randomized alternative trials** in the same context, with at least five trained outcomes for the candidate. Only then can a small ±0.15 bounded bias affect action selection; no action is mandated, and existing exploratory randomness remains.
- The model distinguishes evidence, candidates and confirmed physical crossings. It does **not** contain explicit instructions to pull a gate, infer a locked room, seek a destination or follow a spatial route. The "hypotheses" are statistical action/outcome expectations; truly general goal-oriented planning is future work.

## Architecture and save safety
- The pre-existing `SpatialPerception` exports **read-only** `perceivedX/Y`, `perceivedCellX/Y`, `observedCell` (data already derived from honest rays and body odometry). No privileged `ScarcityLab` gate metadata, target, object type, true map or hidden goal enters the new learner.
- `SensorimotorMind` attaches `CausalExperiments`, snapshots on actual selected actions, passes real energy outcomes and finishes experiments on real elapsed sensory decisions. Keeps `S=26`, physical 16 actuator actions and all old learned neural arrays intact.
- `GameActivity.load()/save()` restores and encodes `causal_experiments_v111` *in the existing working profile*, alongside saved episodic/temporal/body/spatial/physics/statistics data. The old `SaveSlotStore` immutable 12 named slots automatically capture the new key. Old profile files lacking the key start with an empty experimental memory, preserving every previously learned domain. `ResetDomains.CAUSAL` clears only that domain, never the 0.11.0 spatial map or older brains. True death resets only unfinished experiment/candidate cells, **not learned measurements**; pause and scene continuation retain the learned model and mapped life position.
- `BrainToolsActivity` ANALISI adds **ESPERIMENTI / CAUSA ED EFFETTO**, showing self-movement vs environment changes, possible openings, physically verified passages, action alternatives, held-out prediction errors, confidence and choice influences. The separate reset button is clearly scoped. `BrainStatsActivity` counts statistical learning parameters.

## Real phone validation / scientific limitations
**Do not uninstall, clear data or press AZZERA** to update. Preserve existing character in one named slot first, then update with in-app AGGIORNA or the public signed APK above.
1. Open ANALISI → ESPERIMENTI / CAUSA ED EFFETTO. Watch actual experiments and separation of self-motion vs world changes while ZERO moves, explores and touches a movable disk or gate.
2. The newly observed free cells and possible openings may appear **without** verified crossings. That is correct. Watch `Passaggi attraversati davvero` separately; it should increase only on a genuine observed pass.
3. The confidence can remain zero across dozens or hundreds of lives if predictions do not outperform the same-context reference; the module intentionally refuses to steer in that case. **DO NOT** interpret zero as a failure or growing counters as intelligence proven.
4. Close/reopen and switch between different named ZERO characters; ensure the experimental history is independent and preserved, and other prior brains/statistics remain. x1/x5/x20 still use simulated game time, pause does not create offscreen experiences.
5. Compare 25 lives with previous 25 and, preferably, matched difficulty/scene: actual room crossing, energy obtained, damage per simulated minute, newly explored area, physical displacement and meaningful reuse. The simulation may generate different worlds, so life stats comparisons are observational and sensitive to randomness.

**Causal inference limitation:** randomized motor attempts strengthen evidence but comparisons are neither fully controlled trials nor counterfactual proofs; shifts between observed occupancy cells can be affected by sampling resolution, partial occlusion and spatial memory quantization. A provisional opening is not synonymous with actual physical ability to traverse. No semantic understanding or self-awareness is established.
